\section{Text Editing}
You can write \textbf{bold}, \textit{italics}, or \texttt{inline code}. 
For Romanian diacritics, you can type them directly if your editor is UTF-8: ă, î, â, ș, ț.

\section{Mathematical Formulas}
Inline math looks like this: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$. 
Numbered equations for formal papers:
\begin{equation}
    E = mc^2 \label{eq:einstein}
\end{equation}

And aligned equations (perfect for derivations):
\begin{align}
    f(x) &= (x+1)^2 \\
         &= x^2 + 2x + 1
\end{align}
